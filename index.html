The project

Made with editorial care.

The channel takes a non-denominational approach, distinguishing the biblical text from interpretation and illustrative reconstruction.

01 / SOURCES

Stories with references

The workflow builds original English scripts from selected biblical passages and keeps references available for editorial review.

02 / PRODUCTION

Illustration and narration

AI-assisted illustrations, synthetic narration, captions, and original editorial work bring each story into short and longer video formats.

03 / REVIEW

An owner-led launch

The first ten live videos require owner approval. Private upload checks and quality gates help verify the initial publishing workflow.

Google account access

A read-only connection
for the channel owner.

The application uses YouTube API Services to identify the connected channel, check the metadata and processing status of its uploads, and retrieve YouTube Analytics reports such as views, watch time, and engagement counts.

The owner grants access on Google’s consent screen. This Google connection cannot upload, edit, or delete videos. Video delivery and scheduling use the owner’s separate Publer connection.

Account access can be revoked in Google Account permissions. The privacy policy explains data use, storage, service providers, and deletion requests.
